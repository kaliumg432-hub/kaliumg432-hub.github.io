Motor insurance fraud detection

Final-year research project, JKUAT, and my audit of it

Our group built a stacked ensemble of XGBoost, an LSTM and a graph neural network to flag fraudulent motor insurance claims, and reported an F1 of 85.3%. When I audited the code after submission, that result did not hold up: the training data had no fraud signal, and every high score came from a pipeline bug.

What I did: traced and reproduced each bug, then rebuilt the ensemble without leaks and proved it works on a planted fraud pattern, where it reached the best score that pattern allows.

  • Label leakage: claim narratives written from the fraud label gave a “perfect” text model
  • SMOTE before the split turned pure noise into an AUC of 0.79
  • A row-order bug handed each claim another claim’s prediction
  • A Kenyan validation label built from the models’ own inputs
  • Python
  • scikit-learn
  • XGBoost
  • R Markdown
  • LaTeX

ISP customer analytics app

Built for HappyNet Internet Services

An interactive app that replaced a manual spreadsheet process. Load a month of M-Pesa utility account exports and it sorts every customer by product, spend and trend, then suggests what to do next.

  • Separates hotspot and monthly-subscription (PPPoE) revenue and finds customers who use both
  • Tags each customer New, Retained, Growing, Stable, Declining or Churn-risk by comparing daily run-rates
  • Suggests win-back calls, retention calls, upgrades and onboarding offers
  • Answers plain-language questions through an AI assistant that runs the exact numbers locally

Expense control app

Built for HappyNet Internet Services

An app that turns raw payment exports from the company's Tende wallets into a classified expense register. It checks every bill and salary against the payment that settled it.

  • Reads messy, misspelt payment remarks and sorts each payment into a category, with a confidence level
  • Matches rent, internet links, caretakers and payroll to their payments, and flags what is overdue
  • Reads quantities and unit costs from remarks like "5 tenda routers" and flags supplier price rises
  • Tracks fuel against usual amounts, catches possible duplicates, and keeps a full history of every correction
  • React
  • JavaScript
  • Claude API
  • Rule-based classification

Profit First financial system

Excel, built for HappyNet Internet Services

A 10-sheet workbook, with about 2,800 linked formulas, that runs the company's finances on the Profit First method. Every shilling of revenue is split the day it arrives, and spending is checked against what the business can afford.

  • Splits daily M-Pesa revenue into profit, owner pay, tax and operating budgets, and flags overspending day by day
  • Dashboard with pace KPIs, month-end projections and a health summary written by formulas in plain English
  • Owner loan ledger, debt payoff tracker with a projected debt-free date, and a bill calendar ranked by priority
  • Tax compliance calendar for KRA, NSSF, SHIF and NITA, checked against the tax reserve
  • Excel
  • INDEX/MATCH
  • SUMIFS
  • Array formulas
